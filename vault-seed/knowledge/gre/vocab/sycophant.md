# sycophant

> A person who flatters someone powerful in order to gain advantage; a
> servile self-seeking flatterer.

## Examples

- *The CEO surrounded himself with sycophants who never challenged a bad
  idea.*
- *Court life rewarded sycophants more than statesmen.*
- Always pejorative. There is no neutral sense.

## Confusion set

- Not [[sociopath]] — different root, different meaning, similar feel.
- Adjective form: **sycophantic**. Noun: **sycophancy**.
- Compare [[obsequious]] (the adjective for the same behavior:
  "excessively eager to please") and [[unctuous]] (smarmy, oily-charming).
