# laconic

> Using very few words; brief to the point of seeming rude or mysterious.

## Examples

- *His laconic "We'll see." told them nothing and somehow everything.*
- *A laconic reply from a normally chatty colleague is itself a signal.*
- Connotation is neutral-to-positive when it suggests economy; negative
  when it suggests withholding.

## Confusion set

- Not [[ironic]] — different word, common student mishear.
- Near-synonym: [[terse]] (sharper, sometimes implying impatience).
- Near-synonym: [[taciturn]] (habitually silent, a personality trait;
  *laconic* is about the words chosen when one does speak).
- Antonyms cluster: [[loquacious]], [[garrulous]], [[verbose]],
  [[prolix]] — all words for "talks too much" with shading. The GRE
  loves to test whether you can pick the right shade.
