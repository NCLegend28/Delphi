# perspicacious

> Having keen insight; mentally sharp; quick to notice and understand.

## Examples

- *Her perspicacious analysis caught the flaw nobody else saw.*
- *A perspicacious editor knows which sentences earn their place.*
- Usually applied to judgment, observation, or critique — not to objects.

## Confusion set

- Not [[perspicuous]] (= "clearly expressed, easy to understand"). The
  pair is a classic GRE trap: **perspicacious** describes the *observer*;
  **perspicuous** describes the *thing observed*.
- Near-synonym: [[sagacious]] (wise, judicious — closer to "wise" than
  "quick to see").
- Near-synonym: [[astute]] (sharp, especially in self-interest).
